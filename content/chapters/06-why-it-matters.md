# Why it matters

## Story

You've seen four ideas:

1. **A real computer.** Agents that do work, not just answer.
2. **The Parking Lot.** Conversations can wait for days without a computer running.
3. **Sealed Secrets.** Agents that use keys without holding them.
4. **Overnight Engineer.** Agents that remember and keep going.

And one consequence: **anyone can build on it.** Products that need an agent
with a computer don't have to build that infrastructure themselves.

## Takeaway

Fountain is the infrastructure that makes coding agents something you can
build a business on.
